# 已被后续决定替代的方案

以下原文仅保留设计演进证据。五域导航由已接受的完整 4174 八组导航替代；旧价格分类由当前统一成品资料、纯色通货/印刷通货规则替代。不要据此回改当前页面。

- The accepted layered-density navigation direction has exactly five office first-level business domains: `今日工作`, `订单`, `库存交付`, `对账`, and `更多工作台`. Their stable second-level workbenches are `公共待办`; `订单录入 / 订单池`; `库存查询 / 出库交付`; `对账收款`; and `打包/标签 / 原材料 / 客户档案 / 通用价格表 / 规格库存 / 员工机台 / 上线状态`. Wide desktop (`>=1180px`) shows the active first- and second-level locations together in the left navigation; narrow desktop (`768-1179px`, including the required 600px preview) uses an approximately 64px first-level icon rail plus the current domain's second-level strip under the page title with no horizontal menu scrolling. Mobile (`<=767px`) never shows the office desktop menu: phone-first onsite roles use their dedicated role shells, while Office A/B are an explicit exception and use the raw-material-only field utility defined below. Third-level structure stays inside the page as status tabs, workflow steps, detail tabs, or disclosures. Menu changes preserve page-scoped filters, scroll position, and selection; deep links open and select the referenced record; permissions hide unavailable work; counts appear only at the relevant business domain or status tab; unreleased modules sit last and are labeled `后续开放`. Office A/B share the same complete desktop menu and task pool, finance defaults to `对账收款`, and driver/workshop/packing accounts enter their role pages directly.

- The next independent PC navigation redesign review may revisit that five-domain grouping. The user explicitly prefers the structural format and distribution of the earlier `PC 全流程` rail—one vertical column of stable collapsible business groups with indented child workbenches—but this preference is not about its colour palette. The PC information architecture is now evaluated for exactly three desktop roles: `老板 / 办公室 / 财会`; frontline, warehouse, driver, workshop, packing, maintenance, and other onsite roles remain on their dedicated non-PC surfaces. Preserve business continuity and permission-scoped visibility, show the current role clearly, and do not modify production navigation until a generated review direction is explicitly selected and approved.

- ERP general bag-price classification uses two levels.
